Our Story

About Audio Intelligence

We build studio-grade tools that push the limits of what's possible in audio production.

We Build Tools That Inspire

Audio Intelligence was founded by a team of passionate engineers and musicians who were frustrated with the gap between expensive analog hardware and affordable digital plugins. We set out to bridge that gap.

Every product we build goes through months of listening sessions, circuit analysis, and real-world testing with professional engineers. We don't ship until it sounds right — not just on paper, but in the mix.

Our analog hardware is designed in our lab, hand-tested, and built to last decades. Our plugins capture the soul of that hardware and deliver it to studios worldwide.

What We Stand For

Sound First

Every decision we make starts with listening. If it doesn't sound right, it doesn't ship — no matter how good it looks on paper.

Built to Last

We design for longevity. Our hardware is built with premium components rated for 50,000+ hours, and we support our products indefinitely.

Community Driven

Our best features come directly from user feedback. We listen, iterate, and ship updates that matter to working engineers.

Fair Pricing

World-class audio tools shouldn't cost a fortune. We price our products to be accessible to independent artists and professionals alike.

No Subscriptions

Buy it once, own it forever. No monthly fees, no rental traps. Your license is yours to keep for life.

Sustainable Practice

Our hardware is assembled with conflict-free materials, and our packaging is 100% recycled and recyclable.
