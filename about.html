About Audio Intelligence

We build studio-grade tools that push the limits of what's possible in audio production.

We Build Tools That Inspire

Audio Intelligence was founded by a team of engineers and musicians frustrated with the gap between expensive analog hardware and affordable digital plugins. We set out to bridge that gap — with no compromises on sound quality.

Every product goes through months of listening sessions, circuit analysis, and real-world testing with professional engineers. We don't ship until it sounds right — not just on paper, but inside an actual mix.

Our analog hardware is designed in our California lab, hand-tested, and built to last decades. Our plugins capture the soul of that hardware and deliver it to studios worldwide.
